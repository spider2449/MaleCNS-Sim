## Independent MN9 identity evidence and confidence framework

FlyBase term **FBbt:00111298** defines adult proboscis muscle 9 motor neuron: muscle-9 target/rostrum extension, dorsal subesophageal soma, broad mostly ipsilateral ventral subesophageal arbor and pharyngeal-nerve exit. It lists adult `CB0701` and `E49` synonyms. Gordon and Scott (2009), McKellar et al. (2020), and Shiu et al. (2024) underlie this nomenclature. This evidence predates and is independent of Task 018. Muscle target is highly specific but typically outside an EM CNS volume; soma, arbor and exit trajectory are observable in BANC/FAFB if sufficiently reconstructed, and absent from MANC/FANC brain coverage. CB0701 cross-references may be transferred annotations, useful as leads but not independent morphological proof. Laterality identifies the pair's sides, not its cell type.

**ACCEPTED_EXPLICIT:** a release-pinned authoritative native annotation names adult MN9/CB0701 on exactly one complete candidate per biological side, with dataset-native side evidence and no contradictory morphology. A transferred label alone requires source/provenance review. **ACCEPTED_CONVERGENT:** absent explicit labels, two independent anatomical evidence streams must converge uniquely for *each* side: (1) documented pharyngeal-nerve exit or muscle-9 target and (2) subesophageal soma plus characteristic ventral arbor, compared against all plausible proboscis motor candidates; a published morphology match or independent experimental atlas must corroborate at least one stream. A matching name, connectivity similarity, or a single bilateral-looking pair is insufficient. **AMBIGUOUS:** multiple viable candidates or conflicting sources. **NOT_IDENTIFIABLE:** target anatomy is potentially covered but available evidence cannot establish the pair. **DATASET_INELIGIBLE:** coverage or data structure excludes target or endpoint. These rules were fixed without examining candidate connectivity outcomes. Current BANC and FAFB are NOT_IDENTIFIABLE; MANC and FANC are DATASET_INELIGIBLE. There is **no proposed external bilateral root-ID pair**.

## Comparable quantity and access audit

MaleCNS reports directed presynaptic-to-postsynaptic contact counts from its confidence-filtered partner graph; one presynaptic site may have multiple postsynaptic partners. BANC v2 records size-filtered postsynaptic detections (size at least five voxels), and its ordered edgelist aggregates these contacts by pre/post root ID. The segmentation, synapse detector, proofreading and tissue boundary differ. Thus **absolute counts are not defensibly comparable**. BANC is **WITHIN_DATASET_DECOMPOSITION_COMPARABLE conditional on verified target and coverage**: pairwise within-release contact partitions can be calculated, while completeness and left/right proofreading asymmetry must be checked first. FAFB is provisionally **WITHIN_DATASET_DECOMPOSITION_COMPARABLE conditional on mapping and brain-boundary audit**; whole-CNS partner coverage is unavailable. MANC and FANC are **NOT_COMPARABLE for MN9** despite available synapse graphs. Polyadic contacts must be counted as one pre-to-post contact per postsynaptic site, not one presynaptic active zone. Autapses, fragments, confidence flags, duplicate contacts and missing tissue require explicit handling before execution; none was inspected for a candidate endpoint here.

For BANC v888, the static Dataverse deposit and versioned GCS `compiled_data/banc_888/` contain a roughly 188k-row metadata table, ordered v2 edge list and about 218 million v2 per-synapse rows; metadata is approximately 63 MB and full synapse data are large. Morphology is available as SWC/OBJ and registrations. Public static downloads need no CAVE edit authorization; live CAVE queries may need a token. Pin deposit DOI/file versions and hashes, v888 root IDs, v2 products and exact schema; `bancr`, `banc` or CAVEclient and Arrow can read them. MANC can be queried through neuPrint with token or public exports, pinned to `manc:v1.2.1`; FANC through CAVE materialization and annotation/synapse tables, with auth depending on endpoint; FAFB through FlyWire/CAVE or static Codex exports, with materialization and table version pinned. No large data were downloaded in Task 023. Data availability is feasible for BANC; identity evidence is the blocker.

## Prospective comparison protocol, held pending mapping

The following is a **draft protocol specification**, not a preregistration or authorization to calculate. Eligible primary source would be **BANC v888, synapse v2 only if** ACCEPTED_EXPLICIT or ACCEPTED_CONVERGENT identifies its bilateral MN9 pair and the two neurons have acceptable completeness. No source is selected as a primary validation dataset at this gate. Apply the laterality rule above. Eligible presynaptic partners are release-pinned, identified neuronal root IDs with central chemical contacts onto either MN9; exclude autapses, glia, orphan fragments, duplicate synapse records and contacts outside the common imaged CNS compartment. Count each distinct pre/post site contact once, aggregate by ordered pre-root/MN9-root pair, then define presence as **at least one** eligible contact. This no-threshold rule avoids importing Task 018's post-result sensitivity threshold. Declare a separate prospective robustness check using the dataset's documented standard edge filter only if that filter is fixed before seeing MN9 edges; no threshold is chosen here.

For each eligible partner root ID, `shared` means contacts onto both targets, `LEFT-only` means only onto left, and `RIGHT-only` means only onto right. Sum its eligible contact counts per target within each category. The proposed primary quantity is the **within-dataset fraction of total incoming contacts on each target contributed by each of the three categories**, reported with the raw category counts as context. Cross-dataset partner-name mapping is **not required** because categories are formed inside each dataset; it would be required for claims about conserved named pathways, which are outside this protocol. This is a structural decomposition, not a direct comparison of absolute counts.

For a future confirmatory claim, **success** would require that an independently preregistered MaleCNS category-fraction vector and BANC category-fraction vector show the *same uniquely largest category on both target sides*, with both pair maps and completeness gates passed. **Disagreement** would mean different uniquely largest categories on at least one side after all gates pass. **Inconclusive** covers ties, ambiguous mapping or side, missing/unequal target arbor coverage, substantial unresolved input fragments, or unverified synapse schema. These categorical rules do not require matching exact numerical weights. They remain a draft because a release-specific completeness rule, quantified missingness tolerance, and corresponding MaleCNS harmonization cannot responsibly be frozen without first establishing the target pair and source schemas. Do not treat an inconclusive observation as disagreement. Do not evaluate these rules against Task 018 output in Task 023.
