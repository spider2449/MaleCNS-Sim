conclusion does not meet the frozen Task 016 robustness criteria across the
tested model variants.

It does not mean that a biological mechanism is disproven; that biological
network redistribution is false; that direct inhibition is biologically
established or refuted; that any tested candidate is biologically necessary or
unnecessary; or that the result generalizes beyond the tested variants.
`NETWORK_REDISTRIBUTION_COMPATIBLE` remains a model compatibility label, not
biological mechanism proof. A connectome plus model dynamics is not a
reconstructed biological brain.
