## Pre-existing anatomical source freeze

The sources below predate this gate and candidate morphology inspection. Statements are paraphrases of the sources, not candidate observations.

- [Schwarz et al., 2017, *Motor control of Drosophila feeding behavior*, Figure 10 and discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5315463/): MN9 innervates muscle 9 ipsilaterally, has a soma with the pharyngeal motor group, and exits through the pharyngeal nerve. The authors describe it as an exception to their two broad groups.
- [McKellar et al., 2020, *Controlling motor neurons of every muscle for fly proboscis reaching*, Table 1 and Figure 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7316511/): MN9 has ventral SEZ dendrites, dorsal soma, and dorsal/pharyngeal nerve exit; muscle 9 is its target. The same table gives the **same ventral-dendrite / dorsal-soma / dorsal-nerve combination to MN3L and MN5**. Figure 5 shows that ventral dendritic territory is shared by several motor types; its circular gaps are not an MN9-only signature.
- [Gordon and Scott, 2009, *Motor control in a Drosophila taste circuit*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19217375/) and the [Fly Anatomy ontology MN9 definition](https://www.virtualflybrain.org/blog/2022/01/01/proboscis-muscle-9-motor-neuron-fbbt_00111298/): independent historical context for the muscle-9/rostrum motor identity, dorsal SEZ soma, mostly ipsilateral ventral arbor, and pharyngeal exit. The ontology aggregates earlier descriptions; it is not an independent additional vote.

These anatomical statements originate outside the unresolved BANC `wilson_lab` rows. Their *literature provenance* is independent of that marker. Whether an individual BANC skeleton matches them has not been tested.
